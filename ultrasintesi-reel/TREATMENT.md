# Ultrasintesi — rebrand intro · 7 × 6 s · Group 06 (Graphic & Type)

**Brief.** A 6-second opener announcing the Ultrasintesi rebrand (revealed 17 Nov 2026), made in every
Graphic & Type style except Text Flash Cuts ✦ (it needs an image generator; skipped by choice).
1920×1080, silent (the user adds their own track). No narration, so no subtitles.

**Fixed content in every film**
- The wordmark: lowercase **ultra** (Instrument Sans 700) + **sintesı** (400), dotless final ı, its dot a
  Blu Ultra `#2F3DF2` point. The dot is the protagonist in every film: the brand's one point of colour.
- The three disciplines: *graphic · motion · editorial*.
- The claim: **Il tuo ultra, a fuoco.** (IT, above) / *Your ultra, in focus.* (EN, below).
- Movement follows the brand too: the film ends by *subtracting* to a calm lockup that holds still (~30 %
  of the running time).

## Three candidate structures (shared by all seven)

1. **List that turns** — the three disciplines arrive one by one, then collapse into the wordmark.
   Clear and reads fast, but on its own it's a slide deck.
2. **The point that becomes a mark** — a single blue point travels through the style's world and
   lands as the dot on the ı. Strong and on-brand ("a fuoco": everything converges on one point), but
   with no disciplines it says nothing about what the studio does.
3. **Subtraction** — a full, busy composition that is cut away until only the wordmark is left. Very
   on-brand ("togliere"), but busy-then-empty halves the screen time of each element.

**Chosen: 2 carrying 1, ending with 3.** The blue point is the thread (0.0–3.4 s). The three disciplines
build on screen along its path. A subtraction clears the frame and the point lands on the ı (≈ 3.2 s).
The lockup and claim then hold still to 6.0 s. Each style gets its own version of that path.

**Arc (every film):** setup = one point in an empty frame → turn = the style's world builds the three
disciplines → ending = everything is subtracted, and the point lands on the wordmark and holds.

**Reading times (readcheck):** the wordmark and the claim are up ≥ 2.7 s. The discipline words build
up and stay together rather than flashing one after another.

## Beat sheet (all at 120 bpm: one beat = 0.5 s, 12 beats)

| Time | Beats | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.5 | 1 | The point appears (cold open in the style's world) |
| 0.5–2.5 | 2–5 | The world builds; *graphic*, *motion* and *editorial* land on beats 2, 3 and 4 and stay |
| 2.5–3.2 | 6 | Subtraction: cut, split, slide, freeze or zoom clears the frame |
| 3.2–6.0 | 7–12 | The wordmark lands (beat 7) and the claim follows (beat 7.5); everything holds still, with only a breath of motion (sheen, sky, stars) |

Frame rates: 24 fps (Swiss, Spy, Deco, Pictogram, Scale Zoom), 30 fps (Bauhaus, Silhouette), as
their STYLE.md files set.

## Type

- The wordmark is always Instrument Sans (it's the logo). The claim and the micro-text use each style's
  own type roles: Instrument Sans (Swiss), League Gothic / League Spartan (Spy), Josefin Sans / Poiret
  One (Deco), Inter Tight 900 + DM Mono (Pictogram), Jost (Bauhaus, Scale Zoom), Anton / Barlow
  Condensed (Silhouette).
- Corner micro-text in DM Mono caps wherever the style allows it ("luce + materia → forma", studio,
  section, grid coordinates). No invented place coordinates: the readout gives positions on the grid.

## Sound

Silent by request. The cuts and snaps still sit on the 120 bpm grid, so a track at 120 bpm (or 60/240)
locks to them.
